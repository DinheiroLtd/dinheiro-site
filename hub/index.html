Bank Holidays

Upcoming public holidays across your currencies.

Bank holidays in these markets can affect settlement timing for payments due on or around these dates, which is useful to know ahead of Christmas, Easter and long weekends.

A general note on timing: banks and payment systems in the relevant country are typically closed on these dates, which can push same-day or next-day settlement to the next working day. If you have a time-sensitive payment near any of these dates, check with your relationship manager for the exact cut-off that applies.

China's National Day holiday, one of the country's two "Golden Week" breaks. Chinese banks are closed for the full week, so CNY payments sent to Chinese banks around this period typically won't settle until 8 October. Worth planning any China-bound payments around this well in advance.

Introducer Programme

Know someone who'd benefit from a better rate?

Our Introducer Programme is built for professionals whose clients regularly move money across borders: solicitors, accountants, financial advisers, estate agents and mortgage brokers. Earn ongoing revenue share for every client you introduce, with zero conflict and full transparency.

Ongoing Revenue Share

Earn a share of the spread on every trade your client makes, for as long as they remain a Dinheiro client, not just a one-off referral fee.

Monthly Payments

Revenue share is calculated and paid out to you every month, with a full statement so you can see exactly what you've earned.

No Conflict With Your Client

Your revenue share is paid from our revenue, not from your client's rate. They get the same competitive rate either way.

No Compliance Burden on You

You don't need FCA permissions or FX expertise to introduce clients. We handle all compliance, KYC and AML checks on our side.
