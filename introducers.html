Why partner with Dinheiro?

We have designed our programme to be genuinely valuable for introducers, not an afterthought.

Ongoing Revenue Share

You earn revenue share on every trade your referred client makes, not just their first transaction. As long as your client remains with Dinheiro, you receive a share of our revenue. Revenue share is paid monthly with a full breakdown.

Full Transparency

You receive a dedicated introducer portal where you can see every client you have referred, their trading activity (anonymised where required), and your revenue share calculations in real time. No black boxes.

FCA Compliant Payment Structure

Our programme has been structured with regulatory compliance at its core. We handle all FCA requirements on our side, and we provide you with clear documentation for your own compliance records.

Dedicated Partner Manager

You are assigned a named partner manager who is your single point of contact. They onboard you, train your team if required, and are available to answer questions at any time.

No Compliance Burden on You

You are not required to become an foreign exchange expert or hold any additional regulatory permissions to introduce clients to us. We handle all compliance, KYC, and AML checks on our side.

Enhances Your Client Proposition

Introducing your clients to Dinheiro adds genuine value to your service. Rather than telling a client to "use a currency broker," you are introducing them to a dedicated relationship manager who will treat them exceptionally well.

A structured programme with minimal administrative burden.

We have made the process of becoming an introducer, and staying one, as friction-free as possible.

Register Online

Complete our short introducer registration form. We review your application and get back to you within one business day.

2

Sign Your Agreement

We send you our Introducer Agreement, a straightforward document outlining revenue share rates, terms and compliance requirements.

3

Introduce Your Clients

Refer clients by phone, email, or through your unique referral link. We take it from there, contacting your client and onboarding them directly.

4

Earn Every Month

Revenue share is calculated monthly and paid directly to you. You receive a full statement showing which clients traded and how your revenue share was calculated.

Competitive, transparent, and ongoing.

Our revenue share structure is designed to reward introducers who build long-term client relationships with us. The more your clients trade, and the longer they stay, the more you earn.

We do not operate on a one-off referral fee model. Every trade your client makes generates a revenue share payment for you, meaning your introducer income grows as your clients grow.

Revenue share rates are agreed individually and disclosed in your Introducer Agreement. They vary based on the volume and type of business you introduce. Our partner manager will discuss the specific rates applicable to you during onboarding.

Revenue share on every trade

You earn a percentage of our revenue on each transaction your referred client makes, including spot trades, forwards and regular payment setups.

Monthly payments

Revenue share is calculated at the end of each calendar month and paid to you by the 15th of the following month. Full statement provided with every payment.

Volume uplift tiers

As the total volume of business you introduce grows, your revenue share rate increases. We reward our most productive introducers with preferential terms.

Full audit trail

Every revenue share payment is supported by a detailed statement. You can reconcile your earnings against our records at any time through your partner portal.
