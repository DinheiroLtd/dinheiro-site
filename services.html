Spot Transactions

A spot transaction is the most straightforward form of currency exchange: you agree a rate today and settle within two business days. It is the ideal solution for immediate payment requirements: supplier invoices, property deposits and one-off transfers.

Your dedicated relationship manager provides a live, competitive rate the moment you need one. No call centre. Just a rate, agreed in seconds, from someone who knows your account.

Same-day or T+3 settlement

Choose same-day (value today), next-day (value tomorrow), or standard three-day settlement depending on your urgency.

Forward Contracts

A forward contract allows you to lock in an exchange rate today for a transaction that will settle at a specified date in the future, anywhere from one week to 24 months ahead.

For businesses with known future currency commitments, forwards eliminate the uncertainty of rate movements. For property buyers, a forward secures your rate the moment contracts are exchanged, protecting you from the market moving against you before completion.

Enquire About Forwards →

Up to 24 months forward

Fix your rate for transactions up to two years in the future, giving your business or purchase complete certainty.

Small deposit required

Forwards are entered with a small initial margin deposit, typically 5 to 10% of the contract value, with the remainder due at settlement.

Window forwards available

A window forward allows drawdown of funds at any point within a specified period, giving flexibility around exact settlement dates.

The advantages of working with Dinheiro are material. These are the key differences between us and a high-street bank.

Zero transfer fees

Dinheiro charges no transfer fees on any international payment. High-street banks levy between £15 and £40 per transaction. Our clients pay nothing.

A named relationship manager

Every client is assigned a dedicated relationship manager with in-depth knowledge of their business, currency risk profile, and payment requirements.

Same-day settlement available

Eligible transactions can be settled on the same business day, ensuring funds arrive when required rather than within the 1 to 5 day windows typical of banks.

Forward contracts up to 24 months
