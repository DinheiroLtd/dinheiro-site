FAQs

Common questions from brokers.

The questions we hear most often from Dinheiro's broker network. Can't find your answer? Book a call and ask the team directly.

Placeholder content: these answers are generic examples. Replace them with your actual policies before this goes live.
How do I get set up as a Dinheiro broker?
Your Dinheiro account manager will guide you through a short onboarding process, including the New Broker Induction session on the Training Programs tab.
How and when is commission paid?
Commission terms are agreed individually with each broker. Speak to your account manager, or book a call, for the specifics that apply to you.
Where can I find training materials?
All current sessions are listed on the Training Programs tab. Sales and onboarding materials are on the Resources & Downloads tab.
Who do I contact for onboarding support?
Your Dinheiro account manager is your first point of contact. You can also book a call directly from this hub.
Can I refer clients outside the UK?
In many cases, yes. Eligibility depends on the client's jurisdiction, so check with your account manager before referring an international client.
