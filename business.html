Five steps to a live account.

Opening a business account is free, takes under five minutes, and comes with no obligation to trade.

1

Open a Free Account

Complete our online registration form. There are no credit checks, no fees, and no obligation to proceed at this stage.

2

Meet Your Relationship Manager

We assign you a dedicated foreign exchange relationship manager who calls you to understand your requirements and timeline.

3

Lock In Your Rate

When you are ready to transact, your relationship manager provides a live, competitive rate. Once agreed, your rate is secured.

4

Settle Your Account

Send your funds to us in accordance with the agreed settlement date. Our team confirms receipt and initiates the transfer.

5

Funds Delivered

We deliver the converted funds directly to your beneficiary, same-day or next-day in all major corridors.
