How we hire.

We keep our process straightforward and respectful of your time. From first contact to offer, most roles are completed within two to three weeks.

1

Apply

Send your CV and a brief cover note via the form below or by email to careers@dinheiro.uk. We review every application personally.

2

Initial Call

A 30-minute conversation with our HR lead to understand your background, motivations and what you are looking for. We will also tell you more about the role and the team.

3

Interview

A structured interview with the hiring manager and one other senior team member. For specialist roles, this may include a short technical element relevant to the position.

4

Offer

If it is a good fit on both sides, we move quickly. Subject to standard reference and background checks, we aim to have an offer with you within 48 hours of a final interview.
